About DT

DT is a popular clothing brand that combines simple streetwear style with water-protection technology. Our products are designed for everyday comfort and changing weather.

Our Technology

Water-repellent: Helps water droplets roll off the surface. Suitable for light rain and splashes.

Waterproof: Designed to prevent water from passing through the material and provide better rain protection.

Comfortable: Designed to provide a comfortable fit and feel while maintaining water protection.
